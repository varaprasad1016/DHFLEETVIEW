DH FleetView is a fleet tracking and telematics platform for businesses. Monitor your vehicles live on the map, review trips and history, check driver and tachograph status, and view on-board camera footage from your phone.

Features
- Live GPS tracking of your whole fleet
- Trip history and route playback
- Driver and vehicle status at a glance
- Tachograph and compliance information
- On-board camera footage where fitted
- Secure login for your organisation

DH FleetView connects to your DH FleetView account. An active account is required to use the app.
