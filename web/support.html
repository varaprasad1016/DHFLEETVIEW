What to include

It helps us answer quickly if you tell us your company name, the vehicle registration involved, what you expected to happen, and what happened instead. A screenshot is always welcome.

Common questions

How do I get an account?

Accounts are created for you by DH FleetView when your fleet is set up — you cannot sign up inside the app. Your transport manager or account contact will give you your sign-in details. If you need access, email us and we will arrange it.

I have the app but cannot sign in

The app is for existing customers. If your details are not working, check with your transport manager that an account has been created for you, then email us.

Do I need any equipment?

Seeing a vehicle on the map requires tracking hardware fitted to it, supplied and installed by us as part of your fleet agreement. The app itself is free to download and is the way you view a fleet that is already set up. Nothing is sold inside the app.

What does it cost?

Hardware, installation and the monthly service are agreed with your fleet in advance and invoiced directly by us. There are no purchases, subscriptions or payments of any kind inside the app. Contact us for a quote for your fleet.

The map shows no position for a vehicle

A vehicle reports when it has power and mobile signal. A unit parked in an underground bay or a steel-clad yard may show its last known position until it moves. If a vehicle has not reported for more than a day, let us know the registration.

Live video will not open

Camera footage is streamed from the vehicle itself, so the vehicle must be powered and connected. Footage cannot be retrieved while a vehicle is switched off.

Tachograph and driver hours

Driver cards and vehicle unit files are analysed for drivers' hours and working time. If a report looks wrong, send us the driver name and the week in question and we will check it against the source file.

Deleting your data

Fleet and driver records belong to the operator who holds the account. If you want data removed, ask your transport manager to contact us, or email us directly.

Privacy

DH FleetView records vehicle position, journey history and, where cameras are fitted, video from the vehicle. This data belongs to the operating company and is used for fleet management and legal compliance with drivers' hours rules. For a copy of our privacy notice, email us.
